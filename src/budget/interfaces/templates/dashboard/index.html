One Alpine scope owns the toggles, both charts and the fetch, because they are one interaction: changing the period re-queries both series, and the grouping switch chooses which of the two is on screen. The endpoint is lifted from a `data-*` attribute in `x-init` rather than interpolated into the `x-data` body: that body is a JavaScript expression, and `test_user_data_is_not_interpolated_into_javascript` requires anything placed there to be argued for first.
